FAQ

Got Questions?

What educational tools do you offer?

Through our Smart Classroom portal, we provide a wide range of tools including Calculus Calculators, EWU Admission model tests, Visual Bengali Typing trainers, and specialized Programming Contest Series.

Are there resources for language learning?

Yes! We feature unique language apps like Comic Linguist for engaging study, comprehensive IELTS vocabulary lists, and interactive dictionaries to accelerate your communication skills.

Can I access interactive simulators?

Absolutely. We believe in learning by doing. You can access immersive physics, math, and logic simulators to interact with core visual concepts rather than just reading textbooks.

Is there support for university students?

Yes, our digital hub is integrated with the EWU Students Desk, job portals, and comprehensive free university course materials, assignments, and lecture notes for local students in Bangladesh.
